Solville Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 November 2026

Solville (pt.solal.solville) is developed by Solal Studios. This policy explains what data the game handles.

Summary

  • No account or sign-in required.
  • No first-party analytics or tracking.
  • Solal Studios does not collect, store, or sell personal data.
  • The free mobile edition uses Google AdMob for optional rewarded ads (configured for non-personalized, child-directed, G-rated ads without Advertising ID access) and store billing for optional in-app purchases.

Data stored on your device

Your farm save data, unlocked trophies, audio and language preferences, and purchase entitlements are stored locally on your device. They are never sent to Solal Studios. Uninstalling the game or clearing its data deletes them.

Third-party services

  • Google AdMob (free mobile edition): the free Android and iOS editions use Google AdMob to display optional rewarded ads. Solville configures AdMob for child-directed, non-personalized, G-rated ads and removes Android Advertising ID permissions (AD_ID / ACCESS_ADSERVICES_AD_ID). Google may still process basic technical data (such as IP address and device/app information) as necessary to deliver and measure non-personalized ads and prevent fraud. The Desktop / Steam Full Edition does not include ads.
  • Google Play / Apple App Store: optional in-app purchases are processed by your device's app store. Solal Studios does not receive your payment or billing details.
  • Steam (PC edition): if you play via Steam, achievement unlocks and save files (if Steam Auto-Cloud is enabled) are handled by Valve under the Steam Privacy Policy Agreement.

Children

Solal Studios does not knowingly collect personal data from anyone, including children. Mobile ads are restricted to non-personalized, G-rated, child-directed content without Advertising ID collection.

Changes

We may update this policy; changes will be posted on this page.
